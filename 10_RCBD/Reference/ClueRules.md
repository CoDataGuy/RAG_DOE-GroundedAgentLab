# CLUE Classic Detective Game Rules

For 3 to 6 players / Ages 8 to adult  
Rules ©1986 Hasbro, Inc. Printed in U.S.A.

## Objective
Mr. Boddy—apparently the victim of foul play—is found in one of the rooms of his mansion.  
To win, you must determine the answers to these three questions:
- Who done it?
- Where?
- With what weapon?

## Equipment
- **Clue® Game Board**: Shows nine rooms in Mr. Boddy’s mansion
- **6 colored tokens**, each representing one of the Suspects:
  - Colonel Mustard — yellow
  - Miss Scarlet — red
  - Professor Plum — purple
  - Mr. Green — green
  - Mrs. White — white
  - Mrs. Peacock — blue
- **6 miniature weapons**: Rope, Lead Pipe, Knife, Wrench, Candlestick, Revolver
- **Pack of cards**: One card for each of the six Suspects, six Weapons, and nine Rooms
- **Detective notebooks**
- **Confidential “Case File” envelope**
- **1 die**

## Setup
1. Look on the board for the START space and Suspect name nearest you. Take that Suspect token as your playing piece and place it on that space.  
   If fewer than six players are playing, place the remaining token(s) on their corresponding name spaces.
2. Place each of the weapons in a different room. Select any six of the nine rooms.
3. Place the empty envelope marked **“Case File CONFIDENTIAL”** on the “X” in the center of the board.
4. Sort the cards into three groups: **Suspects**, **Rooms**, and **Weapons**. Shuffle each group separately.  
   Without looking, place the top card from each group into the envelope.
5. Shuffle the remaining cards together and deal them face down clockwise around the table.
6. Take a detective notebook sheet and privately check off the cards in your hand.
7. Miss Scarlet (red token) always goes first. Play continues to the left.

## Game Play

### Moving Your Token
On each turn, try to reach a different room of the mansion.

You may move by:
- Rolling the die, or
- Using a Secret Passage (from a corner room)

#### Rolling
- Move horizontally or vertically (not diagonally)
- You may change directions during your move
- You may not enter the same square twice in one turn
- You may not land on a square occupied by another suspect

### Secret Passages
Rooms in opposite corners are connected by Secret Passages.
- If you start your turn in one, you may use it instead of rolling
- Announce your intent, then move to the opposite corner room

### Entering and Leaving a Room
- Enter through a door or via a Secret Passage
- Doors are openings in the wall, not the space in front
- Do not count the doorway as a movement space
- You may not pass through a blocked door
- Stop moving immediately upon entering a room
- You may not re-enter the same room in a single turn
- If trapped, you must wait until a door is unblocked

### Making a Suggestion
After entering a room, you must make a Suggestion:
- Name a Suspect, Weapon, and the Room you are in
- Move the named Suspect and Weapon into the room

Example:
> “I suggest the crime was committed in the Lounge by Mr. Green with the Wrench.”

Rules:
- You must be in the named Room
- All Suspects and Weapons are valid, including your own

### Proving a Suggestion True or False
- Starting with the player to your left, opponents try to disprove your Suggestion
- If a player has one of the named cards, they must show **one** of them to you privately
- If no one can disprove it, you may end your turn or make an Accusation

### Making an Accusation
When you believe you know the solution:
- Say: “I accuse (Suspect) in the (Room) with the (Weapon).”
- Privately check the Case File envelope

Rules:
- You may name any Room
- You may make only one Accusation per game

### If Your Accusation Is Incorrect
- Return all cards to the envelope
- You may no longer move or win
- You must still disprove Suggestions
- If blocking a door, move your token into the room

## Winning
You win if all three cards you named are in the Case File.
Reveal them to all players.

## Special Notes About Suggestions
1. You may name cards from your own hand
2. You may make a Suggestion and Accusation on the same turn
3. Only one Suggestion per room entry
4. Suspects or Weapons already in a room may be named
5. If you were moved by another player’s Suggestion, on your turn you may:
   - Move normally, or
   - Make a Suggestion without rolling
6. Any number of Suspects or Weapons may occupy the same Room
